Emergency Pot = Monzo Cash ISA. Every new Monzo roundup transferred into the Emergency Pot is treated as a fresh Cash ISA subscription: Aurora increases Monzo Cash ISA used and reduces the remaining normal ISA allowance by the same amount. Interest earned inside the ISA does not count as a new subscription.
